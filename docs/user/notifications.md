# Notifications and disk space

The bell in the left rail is the **inbox**: crashes (with what the restart policy did),
failed backups, other failed tasks and — if you turn it on — player joins. Click an entry
to open its server.

Settings → **Notifications** decides per event whether it goes to the inbox and/or appears
as a Windows desktop notification.

## Low disk space

Every 5 minutes MCPanel checks the drives that hold your servers and backups. When one has
less than 5 GB or 5 % free you get one notification for that drive (again only after it
recovered). Servers cannot start with less than 512 MB free.

Each server's Backups tab shows its **disk usage**: worlds, plugins/mods and config, logs
and crash reports, other files, backups, and the free space on its drive.
